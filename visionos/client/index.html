Blueprint

Scan to draw the first blueprint.
  • unobstructed: the scan saw nothing in the way here
  • obstructed: something to walk into stands here
  • unknown: not in view
  • a thing that obstructs
  • a thing that does not
  • a wall, as far as where it meets the floor (~); dashed with ? when the floor line is out of view
  • faded: seen in one frame only · ?: distance unknown

Starts the camera and speaks a description of your surroundings. Once running, tap anywhere on the screen to scan the room, tap Read to read a sign or a label, or tap Ask to speak a question. On a keyboard or the watch: 1 scans, 2 reads, 3 asks, 0 stops speaking. P opens the list of places the app remembers, B the blueprint of the last scan. The camera on the glasses and the voice are chosen for you.
